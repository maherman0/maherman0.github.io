### My Future

As a kid growing up, I was like most kids who were obsessed with playing video games. I started off with Roblox and Minecraft and wanted to become a developer of these games. When I was 6, my parents signed me up for a coding class. Although I didn’t understand what was going on during that class, I thought the things they were teaching was like magic. During high school, I learned more about programming in my computer science classes teaching us about basic python programming. However, although I started to get the hang of the basics, I knew there was much more to learn and accomplish. 

I was interested in Computer Science, but wasn’t sure what to specialize in especially with the diversity of being a programmer. My journey started as an information technology intern where I learned a lot about technology hardware and data entry. However, I knew that my interests were towards programming and making a change in the world. I saw that Hawaii was not known for being a technology hub and many of their students are moving away to seek better opportunities on the mainland and internationally for the latest technology.

I majored in computer science in hopes of bridging the gap between technology and education. I hope to utilize technology such as artificial intelligence to bring up the next generation in learning programming to make new solutions, develop new technologies, and make Hawaii . I hope to create new opportunities, so people don’t need to move away from Hawaii to find opportunities. I want to spend the rest of my time here to not only sharpen my skills to become a better software engineer, but to find like minded students to build better solutions to make this world a better place.
